Here's the whole process

No 40-page report, no jargon. You send data, we do the math, you get a number. Then you decide.

01

We'll sign an NDA first, no exceptions — your revenue is nobody's business but yours. Then export your daily revenue, bookings, or POS transactions, a plain CSV, nothing fancy. Two or three seasons back is ideal, but we'll work with what you've got.

02

We line up every single day you were open against the actual rainfall that day at your location. No guessing, no vibes — just your numbers next to the weather.

03

Which days actually hurt, and exactly how much rain has cost you on them. No sugarcoating, no sales pitch — just the truth about your revenue.

04

If it's worth fixing, we fix it

If the number's big enough to matter, we bring you a hedge built for those exact days and hours. If it's not, we tell you to keep your money.
