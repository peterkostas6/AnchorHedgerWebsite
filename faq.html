Common questions

If something else is holding you back, ask.

What exactly do I need to send you?

A daily revenue, booking, or POS export — a CSV is enough — covering ideally 2–3 seasons, plus your business address so we can pull the matching local rainfall history.

Is my sales data kept private?

Yes — we'll sign an NDA before you send anything over. Your revenue is your business, and it stays that way: it's used only to prepare your report, never sold, shared, or used for anything else.

What if rain isn't actually costing me much?

Then we tell you that. The report is diagnostic, not a sales pitch — if the numbers don't show meaningful exposure, a targeted rain hedge probably isn't worth it to you, and we'll say so.

How long does the report take?

Once we have your data, a few business days for the first pass. We'll walk through the results with you rather than just emailing a PDF.
